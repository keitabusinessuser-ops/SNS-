# Japan Arrival Checklist — Tokyo Edition
**Do these 7 things and your first day in Japan gets easy.**
Last verified: 2026-10-05

## Before you fly

- ☐ **Visit Japan Web** — register for free, and get one QR code for immigration and customs. [F13]
- ☐ **eSIM** — install it at home, and turn it on when you land.
- ☐ **Suica** — iPhone: the Welcome Suica Mobile app (no deposit). Others: a physical card at the station. [F07][F08]
- ☐ **Save these numbers:** 110 (police), 119 (ambulance/fire), +81-50-3816-2787 (Japan Visitor Hotline, 24/7, English available). [F14][F15]

## When you land

- ☐ **Pick your train by hotel area:** east Tokyo (Ueno, Asakusa) → Skyliner from Narita. Haneda → Keikyu or the Monorail. [F01][F06]

## When you shop

- ☐ **Tax-free changed on Nov 1, 2026:** you now pay tax at the store and get it back at the airport. Do the customs check **within 90 days, before you check in your bags.** [F11]

## Before you leave

- ☐ **Tax-free kiosk first, then bag check-in.** No customs check = no refund. [F11]
