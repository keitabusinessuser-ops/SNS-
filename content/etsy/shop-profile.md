
## About（ショップ紹介）
```
First72 Japan makes simple, printable guides for your first days in Japan — starting with Tokyo.

The first 72 hours of a trip are the hardest: new airport, new trains, new rules. Our guides turn that into clear tables and checklists you can read on your phone at the airport.

Every guide shows a "last verified" date. We check prices and rules against official sources, and we update the guides when things change.

Made by a resident of the Tokyo area, with the help of AI tools for drafting.
```

## よくある質問（FAQ）
- **Is this a printed product?** — No. It's a PDF you download right after purchase.
- **Is the information up to date?** — Each guide shows a "last verified" date. Prices marked "approx." should be checked on the official website before you travel.
- **Do you sell tickets?** — No. We only provide information. Buy tickets from the official operators or trusted sellers.
