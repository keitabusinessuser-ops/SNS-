Is the Tokyo Subway Ticket worth it? Quick math 🧮

24h ¥1,000 / 48h ¥1,500 / 72h ¥2,000 (adult)
The cheapest subway ride is ¥178 with an IC card → 5+ rides a day usually pays off.

⚠️ It does NOT cover JR lines (like the Yamanote Line).
⏱️ The clock starts at your first ticket gate, not when you buy it.
