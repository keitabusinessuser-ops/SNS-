Flying to Japan? Skip the paper forms 🛬

Visit Japan Web is free and run by Japan's Digital Agency. You get ONE QR code for both immigration and customs.

It's optional (paper forms still exist), but registering before your flight makes arrival much faster.

Save this for your trip ✈️ Follow @first72japan for your first 72 hours in Tokyo.
