Landing at Narita with big suitcases? The bus can be easier 🚌

Airport Bus TYO-NRT:
• To Tokyo Station from 65 min
• ¥1,500 (daytime, adult)
• Pay with cash or an IC card (like Suica)
• Late night / early morning: ¥3,000

No transfers, no stairs with your luggage.
