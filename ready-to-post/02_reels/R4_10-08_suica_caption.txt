Suica in 2026: app or card? 💳

📱 iPhone → Welcome Suica Mobile app. No deposit, balance valid 180 days, top up with Apple Pay.
🤖 Android or want a card → buy a Suica or PASMO card at the station.

Your card must be in your own name, and some countries have restrictions — test it before you fly.
